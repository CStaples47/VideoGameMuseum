About the Exhibit

Mario Kart takes familiar characters and sets them behind the wheel of go-karts. Racers drive around colorful tracks, collect item boxes and use shells, bananas and speed boosts to get ahead of one another. The driving is easy to learn, but skills like drifting, taking shortcuts and managing items give experienced players an edge.

One clever design idea is that items are handed out based on your position in the race. Players in the back get stronger help, while the leader gets less. That keeps races exciting right up to the finish line, and it lets beginners and experts race together.

Why It Is One of My Favorites

Mario Kart is the game I can hand to almost anyone, from a first-time player to a lifelong gamer, and everyone has fun. It is loud, fast and silly, and it creates laughs every time someone spins out at the last second.
